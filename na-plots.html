Nature Nest · Understanding

What Does "NA Plot" Mean?

"Non-Agricultural" (NA) land status is the gold standard for real estate investment in Maharashtra. It means the District Collector has granted permission to convert the land use from agriculture to residential, making it legally buildable and completely secure for buyers.

With a Gireevan NA plot, you invest with absolute confidence knowing every legal box has been ticked — individual ownership, clear title, and bank approval.
